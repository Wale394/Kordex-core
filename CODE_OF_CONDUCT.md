# Code of Conduct

This project adapts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The complete standard and its terms are available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ . This adaptation retains its goals of an inclusive, harassment-free community and respectful, constructive participation. Attribution: Contributor Covenant, https://www.contributor-covenant.org/, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include clear and respectful communication, constructive technical feedback, acknowledging differing experience and viewpoints, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without consent, and other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional engineering community.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing the project. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and responding to reported behavior. They may remove or decline contributions that violate these expectations. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

There is no published private conduct-reporting contact for this local candidate. The project owner must configure a verified contact route before inviting community contributions; do not place sensitive personal information in a public issue.
